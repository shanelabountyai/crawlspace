# Crawlspace — five-lens review, 2026-11-20

Scheduled run, branch `closeout/2026-11-19` (main 0d27886 plus the closeout docs). Five reviewer agents that had not
seen the closeout work each took one lens (UX and demo flow, visual design, accessibility WCAG 2.1 AA, security, code
quality and tests). A sixth agent, the product owner (PO), who took part in none of the reviews, merged and ranked
the findings. Following the `five-lens-review` skill, the run stopped after the ranking: **no PRDs were written**
(that needs Shane's approval). Seven must-fix items were small and inside this run's rules, so they were fixed with
tests written to fail first (see "Fixed in this run"). Decisions: D-88 to D-99.

## Verdict

**Ship after the top items.** Nothing is a Blocker, and the live product answers. Before the demo and the LinkedIn
post, the risks are one gap in the browser's network guard (rank 2), a few cheap ways to stall the single instance
(ranks 3 to 6, now fixed), and fix-list text that contradicts itself (rank 7).

- **By lens severity (48 findings):** 0 Blocker, 15 Major (UX 4, design 1, a11y 1, security 3, code 6), 33 Minor.
- **By the PO's call (34 work items):** **13 must-fix, 12 should-fix, 9 later**, 0 do-not-do. Of the 13 must-fix,
  **7 were fixed in this run**, 1 is Shane's hand step, 5 are left for Shane (outside this run's rules).

## What each lens found clean

- **UX:** home copy and empty-submit message; progress screen (tab title changes, reduced motion respected); every PDF
  gate state including the 11th request ("Not now."); refused private address; the 100/100 report; operator wrong
  password, sign-in and sign-out; no sideways scroll at 390 px.
- **Design:** every colour is a token, light and dark; status shown as word, glyph and colour everywhere; footer
  placement matches the artboards; the PDF is A4, 3 pages, branded on every page; report hierarchy matches
  `Main.dc.html`.
- **Accessibility:** the reviewer's own axe pass (WCAG A/AA plus best-practice) found 0 violations on 10 screens at
  1280 and 320 px in light and dark, including the signed-in operator page, PDF success and too-many, and the progress
  screen; the only violation at any width was rank 13 (now fixed). Reflow at 320-768 px, text spacing, skip link,
  focus contrast (computed 6.98:1 light, 10.47:1 dark), non-text contrast ≥ 3:1, labels and error association,
  reduced motion, titles, `lang`.
- **Security:** no secrets in the 55 commits checked; every operator route answers 401 without a session; 96-bit scan
  IDs; no emails in URLs or logs; CSV formula guard; PDF text and filename sanitised; no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`;
  redirects re-checked by the SSRF guard on every hop with connect-time DNS checks; 5 MB body cap in the crawler;
  WebSockets and service workers blocked in Chromium; Docker drops to `node` under tini.
- **Code:** N/A checks leave the score alone (CS-05); SSRF errors answer 400; fetcher budgets; interrupted scans are
  failed on restart; migrations in transactions; LLM fallback and 20 s timeout; `markDone` is one transaction.
